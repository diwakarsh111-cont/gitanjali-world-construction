About The Company

Building Trust.
Creating Landmarks.

For over a decade, M/S Gitanjali World Construction has stood at the intersection of engineering discipline and architectural ambition. From ground-breaking to final handover, our in-house teams of engineers, project managers and skilled craftsmen manage every stage of the build — so our clients receive a single point of accountability and a structure that lasts generations.

We believe a construction company is judged not by the promises in its brochure, but by the buildings still standing, strong and sound, decades later. That belief shapes every foundation we pour.
